Follow-up bug fixes and protocol corrections
1. Provenance is recorded for the active YAML, test manifest, and guardian_yolo11n-3 checkpoint.
2. Paired distance metrics use only GT pedestrians matched independently by both models.
3. Distance-bin matched pairs are assigned by ground-truth distance exactly once; unmatched predictions are reported separately by predicted distance.
4. Critical-zone denominators and numerators are printed for all pedestrians and the 2.0 m corridor, with sequence-bootstrap confidence intervals available from the source reports.
5. A clipped-ground-truth matching pass and 30 annotated worst misses were added without changing source labels or checkpoints.
6. Geometry calibration is fit from training labels only and evaluated on the test split.
